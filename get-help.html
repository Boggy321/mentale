💬Need help now?

You do not have to deal with everything alone.

Asking for help is not a weakness. It is a way of taking care of yourself.

Stigma can make it harder to reach out, the worry that asking for help makes you seem weak, or that people will see you differently. That worry is common, and it's wrong. Talking openly is one of the most useful things you can do for yourself.

Who to talk to

Parent or guardian

Teacher

School counsellor

Coach

A friend

A doctor or other health professional

Canadian resources, available right now

Kids Help Phone

Call or text, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Chat is available evenings, 7 p.m. to midnight ET. Free and confidential, but not anonymous[source].

Call 1-800-668-6868 Text CONNECT to 686868

9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline

Call or text 9-8-8, anywhere in Canada, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, every day of the year[source].

Call 9-8-8 Text 9-8-8

Emergency

If your safety, or someone else's, is at risk right now, call 9-1-1.

Call 9-1-1
