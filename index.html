You can't always see what's going on inside.

Mental health is part of your overall health, just like sleep or nutrition. A teen can feel stress, pressure, or loneliness even when everything looks fine from the outside.

Start with Teen Stress Get Help

of 10 to 19 year olds worldwide live with a mental health condition[source]

That's roughly 1 in every 7 teens, including people in your own grade.

Mental health is not about being happy all the time. Everyone has difficult emotions. Having a hard week, or even a hard month, doesn't mean something is wrong with you. But when stress, sadness, or worry stick around and start affecting your everyday life, school, or relationships, that's worth paying attention to.

Why this site?

Most of what makes being a teenager hard doesn't show up in a photo. This site exists to make the invisible part a little more visible: what stress and mental health actually look like, why the people around you matter more than it might seem, and what small things actually help. It's built for a class project, but it's meant to be useful for real.
