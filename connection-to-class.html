Unit 1 and Unit 4

Unit 1, Mental Health: defining mental health, understanding stigma, and telling the difference between everyday stress and a mental health disorder. That's most of this site.

Unit 4, Mental and Wellness Journal: tracking emotional, social, and mental health through journaling. That's where the Wellness Tracker and journal prompts on the Warning Signs & What Helps page come in.
